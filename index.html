🐷 PIGGY CAVE BLAST

A tiny piggy. A big cave. Way too many monsters.

HOW TO PLAY

  • ⌨️ WASD — move your piggy around the cave
  • 🖱️ Mouse — aim your weapon
  • 🖱️ Click / hold — shoot
  • 👾 Defeat cave creatures for points and combos
  • 🏹🪚 Watch out for arrow & chainsaw shooters — dodge their shots!
  • ⭐ Collect glowing bonuses dropped by enemies
  • 🪙 Grab coins that enemies drop
  • 🔫 Find weapon upgrades to get stronger
  • ❤️ Don't let your health reach zero!
  • 🌊 Survive as many waves as you can — mini-bosses show up every 5 waves and will charge at you, so dodge the roll!
